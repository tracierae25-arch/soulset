This Privacy Policy explains how [Your legal name / company] ("we", "us", "Soulset") handles your information when you use the Soulset app and website at soulsetapp.co.uk (the "Service"). We've built Soulset to be private by design.

1. The short version

Your journal entries, gratitudes, habits, moods and name are stored only on your own device — we do not collect or see them. The only personal information that leaves your device is what's needed to take a payment, which is handled securely by Stripe. We don't sell your data, ever.

2. Information stored on your device

When you use Soulset, the content you create — your name, gratitude entries, reflections, moods, habits, intentions and settings — is saved locally in your browser/app storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any server. You can export a copy or delete everything at any time from Me → Export / Reset.

3. Payment information

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. When you start a subscription, you provide your payment details directly to Stripe — we never see or store your card number. We may receive from Stripe limited information such as your email address, subscription status, and the country of your card, so we can provide and manage your subscription. Stripe processes data under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

4. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, your browser/device asks your permission to show notifications. Reminders are generated on your device — we do not send marketing messages through them.

5. Technical information

Our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the Service running, as is normal for any website. We do not currently use advertising trackers. [If you add analytics later, name the provider here.]

6. How we use information

To provide the Service, process your subscription and free trial, respond to your enquiries, keep the Service secure, and comply with our legal obligations. Our legal bases under UK GDPR are performance of a contract (providing your subscription) and your consent (e.g. notifications).

7. Who we share it with

Only the providers needed to run the Service: Stripe (payments) and our hosting provider. We do not sell or rent personal data. Some providers (e.g. Stripe) may process data outside the UK/EU under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Retention

On-device content stays until you delete it. Payment and subscription records are retained by us and Stripe for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet legal/accounting requirements.

9. Your rights

Under UK/EU data protection law you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. As your journal content lives only on your device, you control it directly. For payment-related data, contact us and we'll help. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

Soulset is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll change the "Effective" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
